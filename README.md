# Chaos AI

## Multimodal AI-Powered Study Assistant

Chaos AI is an AI-powered learning platform designed to help students study directly from their own learning material.

Students often have their study material spread across PDFs, notes, screenshots, diagrams, and presentations. Instead of manually searching through these resources or asking a general-purpose AI for answers, Chaos AI allows students to upload their material and interact with it using natural language.

The core idea behind Chaos AI is:

> **Upload your study material, ask questions about it, understand difficult concepts, practice what you learned, and track your preparation — all in one place.**

Chaos AI combines **Multimodal AI, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), Vector Search, Document Intelligence, AI Agents, and Model Context Protocol (MCP)** to create an interactive study environment.

---

# 1. What is Chaos AI?

Chaos AI is a **multimodal AI study assistant** that understands a student's study material and uses it as the primary source for answering questions and generating learning resources.

The system supports:

- PDF study material
- Images and diagrams
- AI-powered question answering
- Grounded explanations
- Source retrieval
- MCQ generation
- Interactive flashcards
- AI-generated study plans
- Learning analytics
- User authentication
- Personal study libraries

The main difference between Chaos AI and a normal chatbot is that Chaos AI does not simply treat the AI model as the source of knowledge.
